# How to play Terminal Tanks

Two tanks, one destructible battlefield, one shot each per turn. Set your barrel angle and power, read the wind, and
hit the other tank. Every hit costs a heart; the last tank standing wins the round.

## Controls

| Key | In battle |
|---|---|
| `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | rotate the barrel (hold `Shift` for big steps) |
| `W` / `S` or `↑` / `↓` | firing power |
| `Space` or `Enter` | fire |
| `X` | give up this turn to charge the **special attack** (only in modes where it is on) |
| `Esc` or `P` | pause |

In menus, the arrow keys (or `W A S D`) move, `Enter` selects and `Esc` goes back. On the main menu, `H` or `Tab`
switches between the home screens you own.

## Aiming

- **Wind** pushes every shell. The arrows on the HUD show its direction and strength, and it changes every turn.
- The dotted **aim guide** shows the flight with no wind; you add the wind yourself. It can be shortened or turned off
  in SETTINGS.
- A blast **right beside** a tank still counts as a hit, and the terrain breaks wherever shells land, so you can dig a
  tank out of cover.

## Special attack and special ammo

- **Special attack**: press `X` to give up your turn and bank a charge (the HUD shows `✦`). With two charges banked (one
  against the Martyr), your next shot is the special: it hits for 3 and has a bigger blast. It is part of the Two Player and LAN rules and
  is always on against the Martyr.
- **Special ammo**: ammo marked `✦` really does something: it bounces, splits in the air, drops bomblets, chains,
  drills, pulls, homes in, changes gravity or blast size, and more. It works in Single Player (on by default), in Two
  Player and LAN when the SPECIAL AMMO rule is on, and always in Terminal Ticket fights. Tournament and Missions keep it
  off so boss fights stay fair; the match banner says SPECIAL AMMO OFF when that happens. The AI follows the same rules.

## Game modes

### Single Player
You against the computer, at EASY, NORMAL, HARD or MASTER difficulty. When you use special ammo, the computer draws a
special ammo too. Wins pay coins (more on harder difficulties) and, from HARD up, a few credits.

### Two Player and LAN
Two players on one keyboard, or two computers on the same network (one hosts, the other joins; the host's rules
apply). Choose BEST OF or FIRST TO a number of rounds, the hearts per tank, the wind, the maps, special ammo and the
special attack. Rock-paper-scissors decides who chooses to shoot first. LAN play is not available in the browser
version.

### Tournament
Twenty boss levels, each opened by the one before. Beating a boss unlocks its exact tank and ammo. A boss may usually
win two rounds (two strikes); its third round win fails the level. Some levels bend the rules:

| Rule | Levels | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| streak | 1, 16 | win the rounds in a row; a boss win resets your streak |
| glass cannon | 5 | your tank has only two hearts |
| flawless | 8 | lose a single round and the level fails |
| gale | 11 | the wind is stuck at its strongest |
| one strike | 14 | the boss may win only once |

Levels 18 to 20 are the big bosses at MASTER difficulty, ending with THE MASTER. Beating the Master unlocks MASTER
difficulty, the Citadel battlefield and the MASTER home screen, and opens Missions.

### Missions
Fifty missions in five acts, open once the Master falls: marathons, 2-vs-1 fights, UFO hunts, co-op battles (with a
friend on the same keyboard, over LAN, or with an AI wingman), boss rushes and, last of all, THE MOTHERSHIP. Every
fifth mission rewards a tank, ammo, battlefield or home screen, and each first clear pays coins and credits. Beating
the Mothership opens Terminal Tickets and the Workshop's HOVER upgrade.

### Terminal Tickets
Sold in the shop once the Master and the Mothership are beaten. A ticket summons one of ten vault bosses at random
(each appears once). You have six hearts; the boss has 9 to 12 and fights with its own special ammo. Win to claim its
tank, ammo, battlefield and home screen, plus coins and credits. A drawn boss stays yours to retry until you beat it.

### The Martyr
When all ten vault bosses have fallen, the shop offers the MARTYR'S TIME MACHINE: a three-phase duel against the
Martyr, with cutscenes between phases. His health is huge (340, 525 and 550), yours is 175 in the first two phases
and 200 in the last, and every hit is worth ten. If a phase goes badly, bring special ammo; Armageddon from the shop
is a good start. Beating him unlocks six legendary tanks, five ammo, three battlefields, six home screens, the ASTRAL
paint and MARTYR'S BLESSING, a shell that wins any duel it hits (Single Player, Two Player and LAN only).

## Coins, credits and the shop

- **Coins (◉)** buy almost everything. **Credits (◈)** come from the Tournament, Missions, Tickets and hard Single
  Player wins, and are needed for master-tier and premium items. Your wallet is always in the top-right corner.
- Every match pays something, even a loss. First clears of Tournament levels and Missions pay the most.
- The **SHOP** has five pages: TANKS, AMMO, MAIN SCREEN (home screens), MAPS and EXTRAS (RGB and animated paints, the
  Terminal Ticket and the Time Machine). Each page goes from cheap to master-tier, with a premium tier at the end.
  Press `G` on an item to open the TANK STUDIO and preview it on any battlefield.
- Cheat codes never add coins; the only way to earn is to play.

## Make it yours

- **Loadout**: in setup, pick your tank, hull colour, ammo and shell colour. Animated paints (RGB, PRISMATIC, VOID,
  MOLTEN, ...) work on any tank once you own them.
- **Workshop** (after Tournament level 5): build your own tank from turrets, hulls, tracks, barrels, glows and auras.
  Each tank costs 2,500 coins; you can keep 24. HOVER (2,500 coins) opens after the Mothership.
- **Home screens**: buy them in the shop or earn them, then switch with `H` on the main menu or in SETTINGS.
- **Jukebox**: every song you have heard or unlocked, with an analyser and a 3-band EQ.
- **Profiles**: up to three separate saves, each with its own progress.
- **Settings**: AI difficulty, wind, animation speed, screen effects, trails, particles, the aim guide, map selection,
  colour mode, audio and volumes, the song pop-up and the home screen.

## Tips

- Shoot into the wind with more power, and with it with less.
- Steep, high shots are easier to correct turn after turn than flat ones.
- If you are dug into a crater, blast the rim away or lob over it.
- The TUTORIAL (main menu) has four lessons on a practice range (aiming and power, wind, ammunition, terrain) and
  three challenges for when you want to test yourself.
